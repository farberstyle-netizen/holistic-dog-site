    Everything you need to know about the HTDA certification, your physical diploma, the national registry, and the ordering process. If your question isn't answered here, contact us directly.

    The Package & What You Receive
    What is included in the $99.99 package?

    Your order includes: a physical diploma printed on heavyweight parchment with a gold foil seal, a leatherette diploma holder stamped with the HTDA crest, a wallet-size certification card bearing your dog's name and unique License ID, a permanent registry listing in the National Holistic Therapy Dog Registry (searchable at holistictherapydogassociation.com/verify), and a digital certificate delivered to your email. There are no hidden fees, no subscription, and no renewal charge during the two-year certification period.

    Is the diploma a physical item that gets mailed to me?

    Yes. The diploma is a physical, printed document shipped directly to your address. It measures 8.5 × 11 inches, is printed on cotton-blend parchment paper, and arrives inside a leatherette holder with magnetic closure. We ship via USPS Priority Mail or equivalent; most orders arrive within 5–10 business days of the order being processed. You also receive a digital version by email within 24 hours of completing checkout.

    What does the registry listing include?

    Each registered dog receives a unique License ID (format: HTDA-YYYY-XXXXX), a searchable public record showing the dog's name, handler name, certification date, certification status, and issuing credential type. The record is publicly accessible through the Verify License search tool. Registry entries remain active for two years from the certification date.

    Qualification & Eligibility
    Does my dog need any special training or certifications to qualify?

    The HTDA certification is a commemorative credential — it does not require your dog to pass an external exam, possess a prior certification, or meet government standards. Any dog may be enrolled. The certification honors the bond between you and your companion and is a meaningful keepsake regardless of your dog's formal training history. It does not confer any legal access rights under the ADA or any other statute.

    Can I certify more than one dog?

    Yes. Each dog requires its own enrollment and receives its own diploma, License ID, and registry record. You can add additional dogs from your account dashboard after completing the first enrollment. Each additional dog is also $99.99 with the same package contents.

    Legal & Certification Scope
    Does this certification make my dog a legally recognized service animal?

    No. The HTDA Holistic Therapy Dog certification is commemorative only. It does not grant any rights under the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA), the Air Carrier Access Act, the Fair Housing Act, or any other federal, state, or local law. Only dogs that meet the specific legal definition of "service animal" under those laws carry the associated access rights — a definition that cannot be conferred by any private organization or purchased credential. If you have questions about legally recognized service animal status, please consult an attorney or the relevant government agency.

    Will the HTDA certification allow my dog into restaurants, stores, or housing?

    No. Businesses and housing providers are not required to accept HTDA credentials in lieu of ADA service animal status. The certification is a ceremonial recognition of the meaningful, positive presence your dog has in your life — it is not a legal access pass and should not be represented as one.
